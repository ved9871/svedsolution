## LinkedIn (founder post)

**Hook:** Google ranks pages. AI recommends brands. Most companies are still optimising for only one of them.

Here's the distinction that clears up the GEO-vs-SEO confusion:

→ SEO competes for a **position in a ranked list**. Driven by on-page relevance and backlinks.
→ GEO competes to be the **brand a model names inside a generated answer**. Driven by entity authority and off-site citation consensus.

Same technical foundation. Roughly 70% of the work overlaps — crawlability, rendering, structure, schema, freshness. It's the other 30% that decides whether ChatGPT names you or your competitor.

The trap: you can hold position #1 on Google and still be absent from the AI answer sitting above it — which is now the thing people read first.

You don't pick one. Sequence them: fix technical foundations, run AEO on money pages, run GEO continuously in the background. Entity authority takes ~90 days to establish and ~9–12 months to become defensible, and it gets more expensive every quarter you wait.
