## LinkedIn (founder post)

**Hook:** "How do I rank in ChatGPT?" — right instinct, wrong word. There is no rank.

A chat answer is generated, not listed. So there's no position #1 to win. What you're actually competing for is whether the model **names your brand** and **cites your page** when it answers your buyer's question.

Two things decide that:

1. **Recall** — does the model already associate your brand with the topic? Built from consistent mentions across sources it trusts. A reputation signal, not a page signal.
2. **Retrieval** — can it fetch your page, extract a clean passage, and attribute it? A technical signal.

The 5-step method we use:
→ Make pages retrievable (server-render, don't block GPTBot, ship llms.txt)
→ Answer the question in the first 100 words
→ Add FAQ / Article / Organization schema
→ Build off-site citation consensus (the biggest lever, and it's off your own domain)
→ Keep it fresh and measure citation share weekly

Timeline is honest, not hype: entity indexing ~30–45 days, first citations ~day 60, consistent mentions ~day 90.
