## LinkedIn
Search is answered now, not listed. Google shows a snippet or AI Overview; ChatGPT replies with a summary that names a few sources. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is how you make your page the answer they use.

The 4 moves:
• Answer first — a direct 40–60 word answer up top
• Question-shaped H2s
• FAQ & QAPage schema
• Capture the snippet + AI-answer slot

AEO is the fastest-feedback AI-search discipline — the same work wins featured snippets in classic search. Full guide (incl. AEO vs SEO vs GEO) 👉 https://svedsolution.com/insights/what-is-answer-engine-optimization/
