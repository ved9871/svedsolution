How we work

Diagnosis first, always. Every engagement runs the same four steps, and the fourth is the one that keeps the results.

  1. Audit

    A 12-point AI eligibility check, a technical crawl, competitor citation reverse-engineering and an interview about your ideal customer.

  2. Map

    An entity map, a topic model, a shortlist of citation sources and a prioritised 90-day roadmap with named owners.

  3. Ship

    We implement: schema, content, internal links, technical fixes and citation placements. Not a slide deck of suggestions.

  4. Refresh

    A written monthly report on citations, clicks and revenue. Then the 30-day loop runs again.

Fair questions

Is GEO actually different from SEO, or is it a rebrand?

Different mechanism, shared foundations. SEO optimises for a ranked list of links. GEO optimises for whether a language model retrieves, trusts and names your brand inside a generated answer. The overlap is technical health and content quality. The difference is entity authority and off-site citations, which classic SEO barely touches. We run both, because GEO on a broken site does not work.

How do you measure AI visibility?

Two ways. Automated citation tracking gives volume and page-level attribution. Alongside that we run weekly prompt testing across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini with a fixed prompt set for your category, logging brand mentions, cited sources and frequency. Manual testing catches what trackers miss.

How long until we see results?

Entity indexing at 30 to 45 days, first citations around day 60, consistent mentions by day 90, stable visibility at 4 to 6 months and category authority at 9 to 12. Ranking improvements often arrive faster, particularly on pages already sitting in positions 8 to 20.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. White-label delivery under NDA with branded reporting, from single audits to full retained delivery. We stay invisible to your client.
