**Goal:** rank in AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) *and* classic organic search.
**Data sources:** Google Search Console export (8-day launch window), DataForSEO Labs, Ubersuggest (Neil Patel). Volumes cross-verified across both tools.

## 1. Executive summary

The site launched around 2 Aug 2026. In its first 8 days it recorded **57 impressions, 1 click, and 0 rankings inside the top 5** for non-brand terms — almost everything sits on pages 6–10. That is normal for week one. It means this is a *land-grab* plan, not a recovery plan.

Three findings drive everything below:

1. **Google is already matching you to the right category.** Without any content push, you appeared for `llm seo` (pos 8), `seo for loss prevention companies` (pos 10), `ai overviews optimization agency`, `ai citation building service`, `ivf seo agency`, `web3 seo agency`, `generative engine optimization agency in india`, and `best ai search optimization platform for a d2c brand in india`. Demand for your exact niche exists and Google sees you as relevant. You just aren't deep enough to rank yet.

2. **Your money pages are too thin to rank or be cited.** All 18 service pages and 9 industry pages carry only **~150 words of unique content each** (the rest is shared navigation and footer). AI engines cite pages with substantive, structured, extractable answers; Google ranks depth. Thin pages do neither.

3. **The AI-search category is high-volume and still winnable.** `generative engine optimization` gets 4,400 US searches/mo, `answer engine optimization` 2,400, `google ai overviews` 22,200 — and the pages currently ranking for the explainer terms are mostly low-authority blogs with 0–6 backlinks. There is a real window to become the cited authority before the category consolidates.

**The plan:** thicken the money pages, then publish a hub-and-spoke blog program that (a) owns the GEO/AEO/LLM-SEO explainer cluster, (b) feeds high-intent commercial terms into your service pages, and (c) is structured specifically to be quoted by AI engines.

## 7. How to make every page AI-citable (GEO/AEO on-page rules)

Apply these to both new blog posts and the thickened money pages. This is what separates "ranks on Google" from "gets quoted by ChatGPT."

1. **Lead with the answer.** Open each post with a 40–60 word direct definition/answer an LLM can lift verbatim. Put it above the fold, before context.
2. **Structure for extraction.** Use question-style H2s, numbered steps, and comparison tables. AI engines pull structured chunks, not prose walls.
3. **Add FAQ + Article + Organization schema (JSON-LD).** FAQ schema maps directly to how AEO surfaces answers.
4. **State facts with numbers and dates.** "In 2026…", "4,400 monthly searches…". Models prefer specific, dated, quantified claims.
5. **Build the entity.** Consistent brand description, author bios with credentials, and corroborating mentions/citations off-site (digital PR) so engines resolve SVED Solution as a trusted entity.
6. **Keep money pages ≥900 words unique**, each answering: what it is, who it's for, how you do it, what's measured, FAQs.
7. **Don't block the AI crawlers you want to be cited by** — audit robots.txt / llms.txt (tie-in with post #22/#23).

*Content-rule guardrails (per project policy): never name clients, no tax/GST details, no promises of specific rankings or guaranteed AI citations, and every published claim carries a real number.*
