## The shift: shoppers ask AI what to buy

Ask a generative engine (an AI tool such as ChatGPT) for the best product for a need. It names a few brands and points to reviews, guides and marketplaces. You become one of those brands through Product and Review schema (code that labels price, stock and ratings), comparison content that answers directly, and consistent mentions on trusted review sites. ## The Kolkata ecommerce SEO method

1. **Fix the catalogue's technical problems.** Control filter menus and product variants, remove low-value pages from Google's index, and make product content fast.
2. **Add shop schema markup.** `Product`, `Offer`, `Review` and `FAQPage` so Google and AI understand price, availability and rating.
3. **Answer buying questions.** Comparison pages and buying guides that open with a direct answer.
4. **Earn mentions on review sites.** A consistent presence on the review sites and marketplaces AI trusts.
5. **Localise for Indian shoppers.** INR, and what local shoppers expect on delivery and returns.
