## The Kolkata local SEO checklist

1. **Optimise your Google Business Profile.** Categories, services, photos and a real routine of posting.
2. **Make your NAP match.** The same name, address and phone number in every directory.
3. **Keep reviews coming in.** A system that follows the rules and brings steady, genuine reviews.
4. **Create area & service pages.** One page for each Kolkata area you serve, with local proof.
5. **Add LocalBusiness schema.** Accurate markup for your service area and opening hours.
