## The London ecommerce SEO playbook

1. **Fix the catalogue technically.** Control faceted navigation and variants, kill index bloat, and make product content fast and server-rendered.
2. **Ship commerce schema.** `Product`, `Offer`, `Review` and `FAQPage` so Google and AI understand price, availability and rating.
3. **Answer buying questions.** Comparison and buying-guide content that opens with a direct answer.
4. **Build review-source citations.** Consistent presence across the review platforms and marketplaces AI trusts.
5. **Localise for UK shoppers.** GBP, UK spelling, delivery and returns expectations.
