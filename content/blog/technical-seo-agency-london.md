Most London websites do not have a content problem. They have a foundation problem. Pages that Google and AI tools cannot open and read cannot rank on Google or be mentioned in AI answers. A **technical SEO agency in London** fixes that foundation so everything else works. Here is what it covers and why it matters more than ever.

## Why technical SEO is the foundation for AI search

AI tools such as ChatGPT can only mention pages they can find and read. If your content only appears after JavaScript runs, most AI crawlers (the programs that fetch pages for AI tools) see an empty shell, and no amount of content or outside mentions will help. Technical SEO comes before both Google rankings and AI visibility, which is why it is the first thing we fix. ## The AI-search angle most agencies miss

In 2026, a technical review that only checks Core Web Vitals (Google's speed and stability scores) misses the point. The new questions are these. Can AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended) fetch your pages? Is your content built on the server? Is your schema markup (code that labels what a page is about) valid and matched to what visitors see? Do you have an `llms.txt` file? The answers decide whether AI tools can use your site as a source at all. They sit underneath [Generative Engine Optimization](/generative-engine-optimization/) and [Answer Engine Optimization](/services/answer-engine-optimization/). ## The London technical SEO checklist

1. **Check what a bot can read.** Fetch key pages as a bot and confirm the real content is in the raw HTML.
2. **Fix how pages are built.** Move important content to server-built or static pages.
3. **Clean the index.** Remove low-value pages created by filter menus and URL parameters.
4. **Fix Core Web Vitals.** LCP, INP and CLS across page templates.
5. **Add and test schema and llms.txt.** Match schema to visible content, then test in Rich Results.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does a technical SEO agency in London do?**
It fixes a site's technical foundations: crawling, how pages are built, Core Web Vitals, site structure and schema markup. These decide whether a site can rank on Google and be mentioned by AI.

**Why does technical SEO matter for AI search?**
AI tools can only mention pages they can fetch and read. Client-side rendering (pages built in the browser), blocked crawlers and missing schema make good content invisible to ChatGPT and AI Overviews.

**Is technical SEO worth it if our content is weak?**
It has to come first, and it still needs good content on top. Excellent content on a broken site does not rank, and a perfect foundation under thin content ranks no better. We start with whichever is really holding you back.

**How do we know if we have a technical problem?**
Common signs: far more pages indexed than you really have (too many low-value pages), content missing from the raw HTML (a page-build problem), or flat rankings despite good content. A review confirms which applies.

**How long do technical fixes take to show results?**
Some fixes (how pages are built, crawling) take effect within days of going live. Others (Core Web Vitals, site structure) build up over weeks.
