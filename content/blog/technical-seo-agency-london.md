Most London sites don't have a content problem — they have a foundation problem. Pages that can't be crawled, rendered or parsed can't rank on Google and can't be cited by AI. A **technical SEO agency in London** fixes that foundation so everything else works. Here's what it covers and why it now matters more than ever.

## Why technical SEO is the foundation for AI search

AI answer engines can only cite what they can retrieve and parse. If your content only appears after JavaScript runs, most AI crawlers see an empty shell — so no amount of content or citation work lands. Technical SEO is the prerequisite for both Google rankings and AI visibility, which is why it's the first thing we fix. ## The AI-search angle most agencies miss

A technical audit that only checks Core Web Vitals misses the point in 2026. The new questions are: can AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended) fetch your pages? Is your content server-rendered? Is your schema valid and matched to visible content? Do you ship an `llms.txt`? These determine whether you're eligible to be cited at all — the foundation beneath [Generative Engine Optimization](/generative-engine-optimization/) and [Answer Engine Optimization](/services/answer-engine-optimization/). (See [what is LLM SEO](/insights/what-is-llm-seo/).)

## The London technical SEO checklist

1. **Audit retrievability.** Fetch key pages as a bot and confirm the real content is in the raw HTML.
2. **Fix rendering.** Move critical content to server-rendered or static output.
3. **Clean the index.** Remove bloat from faceted navigation and parameters.
4. **Remediate Core Web Vitals.** LCP, INP and CLS across templates.
5. **Ship and validate schema + llms.txt.** Match schema to visible content; test in Rich Results.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does a technical SEO agency in London do?**
It fixes the technical foundations — crawlability, rendering, Core Web Vitals, architecture and schema — that determine whether a site can rank on Google and be cited by AI.

**Why does technical SEO matter for AI search?**
AI systems can only cite pages they can fetch and parse. Client-side rendering, blocked crawlers and missing schema make good content invisible to ChatGPT and AI Overviews.

**Is technical SEO worth it if our content is weak?**
It's a prerequisite, not a substitute. Excellent content on a broken site doesn't rank — but a perfect foundation under thin content ranks no better. We sequence whichever is the real constraint first.

**How do we know if we have a technical problem?**
Common signs: pages indexed far above your real page count (bloat), content missing from the raw HTML (rendering), or flat rankings despite good content. An audit confirms which applies.

**How long do technical fixes take to show results?**
Some (rendering, crawlability) take effect within days of deploy; others (Core Web Vitals, architecture) compound over weeks.
