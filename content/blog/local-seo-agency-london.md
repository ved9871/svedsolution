## The shift: Londoners ask AI for local recommendations

Ask a generative engine (an AI tool such as ChatGPT) for the best local service in a London area. It names a few businesses and points to directories, reviews and local content. You become one of those businesses through the same NAP (name, address and phone number) everywhere, a steady flow of new reviews, and LocalBusiness schema, the code that tells search engines your address, area and hours. ## The London local SEO checklist

1. **Optimise your Google Business Profile.** Categories, services, photos and a real routine of posting.
2. **Make your NAP match.** The same name, address and phone number in every UK directory.
3. **Keep reviews coming in.** A system that follows the rules and brings steady, genuine reviews.
4. **Create borough & service-area pages.** One page for each area you serve, with local proof.
5. **Add LocalBusiness schema.** Accurate markup for your service area and opening hours.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does a local SEO agency in London do?**
It wins the Google map pack and "near me" searches and gets the business recommended by AI, through Google Business Profile work, directory listings, reviews, local pages and schema.

**How do local businesses get named by ChatGPT or AI Overviews?**
Through the same NAP everywhere, strong reviews, LocalBusiness schema and local content on the sites AI trusts. These are the same signals that win the map pack.

**Can I rank in boroughs where I don't have an address?**
Not in the map pack, which needs a real presence in the area. You can still rank in the normal Google results for area-plus-service searches with well-built service-area pages. For many businesses that is worth more.

**Why aren't we in the London map pack?**
Usually the NAP differs between directories, the reviews are too few or too old, or the service-area setup does not match how people search. A check shows which one applies.

**How long before local results improve?**
Improvements to your Google Business Profile and reviews often show within weeks. Borough page rankings typically move in 60–90 days.
