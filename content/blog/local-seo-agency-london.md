1. **Optimise Google Business Profile.** Categories, services, photos and a real posting cadence.
2. **Fix NAP consistency.** Identical name, address and phone across every UK directory.
3. **Build review velocity.** A compliant system for steady, genuine reviews.
4. **Create borough & service-area pages.** A page per area you serve, with local proof.
5. **Ship LocalBusiness schema.** Accurate service-area and opening-hours markup.
